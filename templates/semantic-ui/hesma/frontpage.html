HESMA

A searchable archive for model data from supernova, common-envelope, and white dwarf merger simulations.

Simulation snapshots Initial conditions Tracer-particle data Radiative transfer

About the Archive

HESMA collects simulation snapshots, post-processed data products, initial conditions, tracer-particle data for nuclear post-processing, and radiative-transfer outputs.

The archive supports reuse, comparison, and publication workflows for computational astrophysics research.
